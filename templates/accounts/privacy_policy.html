Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

Welcome to IndiKum ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website (collectively, the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as information collected automatically when you use the App.

A. Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your phone number.
  • Profile Information: Profile pictures and other details you choose to add to your profile.
  • Shipping & Billing Information: Full name, phone number, delivery address, and city/region to process and deliver your orders.
  • Supplier Information: If you use the App as a supplier, we collect business details, commercial registration (CR) numbers, and tax information.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: We collect information about your mobile device, including device model, operating system, unique device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), and mobile network information.
  • Log Data: IP address, browser type, access times, and pages viewed.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the App, including products viewed, items added to the cart, and AR (Augmented Reality) features used.

C. Information from Third Parties

  • Social Media / Authentication: If you log in via a third-party service such as Google or Apple, we may receive your name and email from that provider.

2. Device Permissions

To provide specific features, IndiKum may request access to the following device permissions:

  • Camera & Photo Storage: Required for uploading profile pictures, supplier product images, and utilizing the Augmented Reality (AR) "View in Your Room" feature.
  • Location: Used to suggest nearby suppliers, calculate accurate shipping costs, and provide localized content.
  • Notifications: Used to send you order updates, promotional offers, and important app alerts via services such as Firebase and OneSignal.

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device's settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Order Fulfillment: To process payments, arrange shipping, and deliver products to your doorstep.
  • Supplier Matching: To share your shipping address and order details with the specific supplier fulfilling your order.
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries, resolve disputes, and provide technical assistance.
  • App Improvement: To analyze usage trends, fix bugs, and develop new features, including our AR shopping experience.
  • Marketing & Communications: To send you push notifications, emails, or SMS about new products, exclusive deals, and order status updates. You can opt out of marketing communications at any time.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: To monitor for suspicious activity and protect the integrity of the IndiKum marketplace.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others.

We may share generic aggregated demographic information that is not linked to any personal identification information with our business partners.

We share your personal information only in the following situations:

  • With Suppliers: To fulfill your order, we must share your name, phone number, and shipping address with the supplier selling the product.
  • With Service Providers: We share data with trusted third parties who assist us in operating the App, including cloud hosting services such as Firebase, payment gateways, delivery/logistics partners, and push notification services.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities, such as a court or government agency in Saudi Arabia.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information. This includes SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit and secure server infrastructure.

However, please remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We will retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy.

We will retain and use your information to the extent necessary to comply with our legal obligations, including applicable tax and e-commerce laws in Saudi Arabia, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & Update: You can review and update your account information directly within the App's "My Account" section.
  • Deletion: You may request the deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us.
  • Opt-Out: You can opt out of marketing communications by adjusting your notification settings in the App or clicking "unsubscribe" in our emails.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at: support@indikum.com

8. Children's Privacy

IndiKum is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that we have collected data from a child without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information immediately.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us.
